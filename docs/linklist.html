By activating the treaty chain to the NATO Status of Forces Agreement and selling the telecommunication network as part of the internal development, it was agreed to continue operating the telecommunication system. Through the continued use of the international telecommunications grid since 1998, the entire world has partially fulfilled the treaty, thereby obligating it to fulfill the treaty in its entirety and making the treaty irreversible.
