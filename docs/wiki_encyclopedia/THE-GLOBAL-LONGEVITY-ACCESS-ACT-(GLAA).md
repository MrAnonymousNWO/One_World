* **Total Cost Obsolescence:** In the post-scarcity economy, healthcare has no price tag. Medical AI, robotic surgeons, and automated pharmaceutical labs operate as part of the **Global Resource Protocol**.

* **Global Parity:** Whether you are in a remote arcology or a dense urban hub, the quality of medical care is identical. Geography no longer dictates life expectancy.

### 2. THE LONGEVITY PROTOCOL: CELLULAR REGENERATION
Longevity is not just "living longer"; it is **staying young**.
* **Biological Maintenance:** The GLAA provides access to gene-editing, senolytic therapies (clearing out "zombie cells"), and nanobot-assisted tissue repair. 
* **The Age Reset:** Citizens can undergo periodic "Rejuvenation Cycles" to maintain their physical and cognitive peak. This ensures that a 150-year-old human has the vitality of a 25-year-old.
* **Brain Health & Memory:** Advanced neuro-regeneration prevents dementia and cognitive decline, ensuring that the wisdom of the "Centenarians" remains sharp and active in the **Fluent Democracy**.

### 3. PSYCHOLOGICAL IMPACT: THE "CENTURY MINDSET"
The most powerful effect of the GLAA is the shift in human behavior.
* **The "I-Will-Be-There" Effect:** When you expect to live for 200 years, your perspective on climate change, pollution, and space travel shifts from "their problem" to "my problem."
* **The End of Life-Panic:** The frantic "career-climbing" of the early 21st century—driven by the fear of running out of time—is replaced by a relaxed, exploratory lifestyle. You can spend 20 years as a musician, 20 as a physicist, and 20 as an ocean gardener.
* **Sustainable Ambition:** Ambition is channeled into long-term planetary projects (e.g., terraforming Mars or reforesting continents) that require a multi-century vision.
