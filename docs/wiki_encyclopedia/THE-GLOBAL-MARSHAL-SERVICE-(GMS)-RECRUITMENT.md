## Enforcement for the Law, Not the Leader

The GMS is a specialized, decentralized force designed to protect human rights and infrastructure. It operates without political oversight, guided solely by the **Smart Contract Constitution**. Through the integration of non-lethal robotics and algorithmic accountability, it eliminates the "human error" of corruption and brutality.

---

### 1. RECRUITMENT: THE ETHICAL ARCHITECTS
The GMS does not look for "soldiers." It looks for "De-escalators."
* **Psychological Filtering:** Candidates are screened for high empathy, high IQ, and a total absence of tribalist or nationalistic tendencies. 
* **The Oath to the Code:** Marshals do not swear allegiance to a person or a flag. They swear to the **Global Protocol**. If a command violates the Protocol (e.g., "suppress a peaceful protest"), the Marshal's digital equipment and authority are automatically deactivated by the system.
* **Radical Transparency:** Every action, word, and decision of a Marshal is recorded on the public blockchain. There is no "qualified immunity."

### 2. ROBOTIC CO-ENFORCEMENT: THE "GUARDIAN" UNITS
Human Marshals are always paired with **Autonomous Guardian Units (AGUs)**.
* **Objective Observation:** Robots act as "unbiased witnesses." They record 360-degree data, ensuring that the human Marshal cannot lie about an encounter.
* **Risk Absorption:** Because robots are replaceable, there is no "fear-based" escalation. A robot can walk into a dangerous situation to negotiate without the fear of death that often triggers human police to use excessive force.
* **Crowd Calibration:** AGUs use advanced biometrics to detect stress levels in a crowd and adjust their posture and positioning to lower tension automatically.



### 3. THE END OF LETHAL FORCE: NEURO-BLOCKERS & KINETIC RESTRAINT
In the Electric Technocracy, the "Death Penalty" and "Lethal Force" are obsolete.
* **Non-Lethal Mandate:** GMS equipment is physically incapable of delivering lethal damage. 
* **EMP & Signal Interdiction:** Instead of shooting, Marshals use high-precision electronic interference to disable weapons, vehicles, or drones used by aggressors.
* **Stasis Restraint:** For violent individuals, Marshals use rapid-expanding foam or localized neuro-muscular blockers that temporarily immobilize the subject without causing long-term injury. 
* **The "Peaceful Extraction":** The goal is always to remove the threat from the situation, not to destroy the threat.

### 4. DECENTRALIZED COMMAND: THE ALGORITHMIC DISPATCH
* **No "Chief of Police":** There is no central commander who can be bribed or pressured. 
* **Direct Citizen SOS:** Requests for GMS intervention are generated via the **Global Citizen ID**. If a person's rights are being violated (e.g., someone is blocking their access to the Resource Baseline), the system dispatches the nearest Marshal/AGU team automatically.
* **Jurisdiction-Free:** Marshals have "Universal Jurisdiction." They can enter any former national territory to stop a violation of the Global Bill of Rights. No "sovereignty" can block the path of justice.
