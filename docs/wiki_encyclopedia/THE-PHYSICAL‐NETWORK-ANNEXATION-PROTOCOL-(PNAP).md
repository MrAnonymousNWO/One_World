* **The Atlantic Bridge:** European networks are physically linked to North America via deep-sea communication cables.
* **Jurisdictional Jump:** The moment these cables are captured, the United States and Canada are integrated into the territorial expansion.

The expansion moves from NATO members to UN member states through their shared physical connectivity.
* **The Multi-Membership Conduit:** Because most NATO members are simultaneously UN members, the "Domino Effect" passes seamlessly into the UN treaty framework, involving every UN-aligned nation connected to global grids.
