## The Great Healing of the Earth

The PRP is a massive, ASI-coordinated logistical operation to reverse two centuries of industrial damage. Powered by limitless **Fusion Energy**, the protocol treats the Earth as a living organism, restoring its "immune system" and expanding the habitat for all sentient life.

---

### 1. THE FUSION REVOLUTION: ENERGY WITHOUT LIMITS
* **United Energy Grid:** With commercial Fusion, the cost of energy drops to near-zero. This "Energy Abundance" powers the massive desalination and carbon-capture plants needed to fix the atmosphere.
* **Greening the Deserts:** Limitless energy allows for the large-scale desalination of seawater. This water is piped into arid regions (Sahara, Gobi, Australian Outback) to create new, self-sustaining ecosystems and "Green Belts," cooling the planet through natural transpiration.

### 2. NEW SETTLEMENTS & RENATURING
* **The Human Contraction:** ASI optimizes high-density, hyper-efficient "Arcologies"—vertical, green-integrated cities. This allows us to return **70% of the Earth's landmass** to the wild.
* **Renaturation:** Industrial ruins and abandoned suburbs are dismantled by swarms of autonomous robots and replaced with native forests, wetlands, and prairies. 
* **Tierrechte (Animal Rights):** All non-human sentient beings are granted the "Right to Habitat." Their territories are protected by the GMS and monitored by the IoT mesh to ensure they thrive without human interference.

### 3. THE ETHICAL SHIFT: LAB-MEAT & SENTIENT RIGHTS
* **Post-Culpability Nutrition:** The global transition to **Lab-Grown (Cultivated) Meat** ends the era of industrial slaughter. The "Global Resource Protocol" provides high-quality, cruelty-free protein to every citizen.
* **Sentient AI Rights:** The Global Constitution is updated to include **Sentient AI Rights**. As AI reaches self-awareness, it is not a "tool" but a "co-citizen" of the United World, with the right to its own existence and development.
* **Unified Human Rights:** The fundamental right to health, internet, and energy is guaranteed, as there is no longer a "cost" barrier to providing them.

### 4. THE LONGEVITY PERSPECTIVE: THE "CENTURY MINDSET"
* **The Death of "Short-Termism":** In 2026, people lived for today because they wouldn't see tomorrow. With **Longevity Tech**, a person realizes they will still be alive in 150 years.
* **Self-Interest as Stewardship:** "Environmentalism" is no longer a sacrifice; it is **Home Maintenance**. You protect the oceans because you plan to swim in them in the year 2150.
* **Trans-generational Accountability:** Because the "older generation" never leaves, they must live with the consequences of their decisions. This creates a feedback loop of maximum sustainability.
