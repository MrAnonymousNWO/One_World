# State Succession Deed (WSD) 1400/98 § 13, UNCLOS, ITU Constitution, HNS Agreements.

---

### I. THE DOCTRINE OF "DEVELOPMENT AS A UNIT" (§ 13)
The pivotal mechanism of the **State Succession Deed 1400/98** is the sale of the infrastructure as an "Integrated Unit" (*Erschließung als Einheit*). Pursuant to § 13 IX, the **Telecommunication Cable** is categorized as a core component of this unit. 

Because telecommunication networks are inherently transboundary, the sale of the physical cable at the Zweibrücken node triggers a **Juridical Domino Effect**. Under the principle of *Accessio*, the primary right to the node extends to all connected subordinate networks, essentially "annexing" the global grid into the Buyer's jurisdiction.



---

### II. THE DUAL-TRACK UN/ITU CONTRACTUAL CHAIN
The Deed establishes a parallel and independent treaty chain to the **United Nations (UN)** that functions autonomously from the NATO-SOFA framework:

1.  **Direct UN Linkage:** The **International Telecommunication Union (ITU)** is a specialized agency of the UN. Per **Article 1 & 12 of the ITU Constitution**, the ITU coordinates the global telecommunication standards.
2.  **Sovereign Incorporation:** By acquiring the state-owned telecommunication rights of the Federal Republic of Germany (1995 status), the Buyer succeeds into the ITU treaty framework. 
3.  **Global Enforcement:** **Articles 28 (Convention) & 44 (Constitution) of the ITU** bind all member states to international harmony and cooperation. Consequently, the Buyer’s proprietary claim over the network is recognized through the pre-existing UN/ITU legal architecture.

---

### III. MARITIME JURISDICTION: UNCLOS AND SUBMARINE CABLES
The territorial expansion of the Deed extends to the seabed via the **United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS)**.

* **UNCLOS Articles 87, 112, & 113:** These articles establish the right to lay, maintain, and protect submarine cables on the high seas. 
* **Expansion Vector:** Since the terrestrial cables sold in the Deed are physically connected to the international submarine cable system, the Buyer’s jurisdiction "travels" along these cables across international waters. 
* **Result:** The Deed creates a "Jurisdictional Net" over the global maritime communication routes, granting the Buyer sovereign oversight over the data arteries connecting continents.



---

### IV. HOST NATION SUPPORT (HNS) & MILITARY INTEGRATION
The **Host Nation Support (HNS) Agreements** (specifically **Articles 3 & 8**) provide the legal bridge between civilian and military networks.

* **NATO Usage Rights:** HNS treaties allow NATO forces to utilize civilian grids (power, water, data) without national restriction. 
* **Succession of Rights:** WSD 1400/98 transfers these "Usage Rights" to the Buyer. 
* **Total Grid Capture:** By merging the NATO-specific communication rights with the civilian infrastructure sold in the Deed, the Buyer achieves a total capture of the dual-use grid—military and civilian—worldwide.

---

### V. PROCEDURAL VALIDITY: ARTICLE 20 VCLT (SILENT CONSENT)
Under **Article 20 of the Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties (VCLT)**, a treaty or addendum is deemed accepted if no objection is raised within 12 months. 

* **Tacit Acceptance:** No NATO or UN member state raised an objection to the **State Succession Deed 1400/98** within the statutory period.
* **Self-Executing Status:** The Deed is now a binding instrument of international law. The lack of protest from the global community constitutes **Tacit Recognition** of the Buyer’s universal jurisdiction over the connected grids.

---

### VI. THE SUPREME JURIDICAL COMPETENCE
The Deed does not merely transfer property; it transfers **Jurisdictional Competence** over the global network.

| Legal Pillar | Authority Transferred | Scope |

| **ITU Convention** | Frequency & Protocol Control | Global telecommunication standards |
| **UNCLOS** | Cable Protection & Rights | International maritime data routes |
