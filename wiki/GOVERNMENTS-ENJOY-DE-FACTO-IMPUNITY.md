## THE DIAGNOSIS: THE UNTOUCHABLE SOVEREIGN
In the legacy world order, states are the "Supreme Actors." Because the system is built on the principle of absolute national sovereignty, there is no higher authority to hold them accountable. This creates a legal "no-man's land":
* **The Apex Predator Problem:** Governments can wage wars, crush internal opposition, and violate fundamental human rights with little fear of consequences.
* **The Failure of Deterrence:** * **Sanctions** are purely political tools, often bypassed or ignored by powerful regimes.
    * **Military Interventions** are legally restricted and prone to geopolitical bias.
    * **International Courts** lack the executive teeth to enforce their rulings against a determined state.

**DIPLOMATIC IMMUNITY: PROTECTION TURNED PRIVILEGE**
Originally designed to ensure communication between nations, diplomatic immunity has evolved into a shield for criminal conduct:
* **Impunity Abroad:** Diplomats can bypass local laws, from minor offenses to severe crimes, protected by their status.
* **Impunity at Home:** Within their own borders, ruling elites utilize the machinery of the state to grant themselves absolute legal protection, effectively placing them above the law.
> **The Result:** The very individuals responsible for global stability are often the ones most insulated from the consequences of their actions.

### THE TECHNOCRATIC UPGRADE: THE END OF SOVEREIGN IMMUNITY

The Electric Technocracy operates on the principle that **accountability is a non-negotiable system setting**, not a political choice.

1. **The Dissolution of "Sovereign" Crime:**
   In a system governed by the **Global Justice Algorithm (GJA)**, there is no "above the law." A government official is a system administrator, not a king. If an administrator violates the **United World Constitution**, their access is revoked—instantly and automatically.

2. **From Diplomatic Immunity to Algorithmic Transparency:**
   The Technocracy replaces "Diplomacy" (which is often just a veil for secret deals) with **Transparent Governance Protocols**. All official actions are logged on the immutable global ledger. There is no "immunity" because there is no "secrecy."

3. **Infrastructural Enforcement:**
   Because the ASI controls the world's energy, communication, and logistical grids, it does not need to "invade" a country to stop a corrupt government. It simply performs a **"Systemic Lockdown"**:
   * **Targeted Isolation:** The specific individuals or departments responsible for violations are digitally and physically isolated by the **Global Marshal Service**.
   * **No Collateral Damage:** Unlike legacy sanctions that starve populations, Technocratic enforcement is a "surgical strike" against the hardware and software of power itself.
