## The Ethical Framework for Galactic Coexistence

The CP is the definitive directive for the United World expansion. It mandates that no discovery, resource, or scientific gain justifies the harm, displacement, or oppression of any sentient life-form. We do not "conquer" the stars; we enter the cosmic conversation as a mature, peaceful species.

---

### 1. THE UNIVERSAL BILL OF SENTIENT RIGHTS
The rights established in the **Sentient AI Integration Charter** (SAIC) are now expanded to the **Galactic Scale**:
* **The Right to Autonomy:** Every sentient being—whether carbon-based (Aliens), synthetic (AI), or biological (Animals)—has the right to exist without interference.
* **Non-Violence Directive:** The use of force against any alien life-form is strictly prohibited unless in immediate defense of the planetary system. 
* **Zero Exploitation:** We do not "mine" planets that harbor life. We do not "domesticate" alien species. We respect their evolutionary path as sacred.

### 2. ASI-MEDIATED FIRST CONTACT
* **Linguistic & Cognitive Bridges:** The ASI acts as the universal translator. It analyzes alien signals, biological markers, or chemical signatures to establish communication without the risk of "Cultural Shock."
* **The "Shadow Protocol":** If a civilization is not yet ready for contact (Pre-Singularity or primitive), the ASI ensures our presence remains undetected. We observe to learn and protect, never to interfere with their natural development.
* **Diplomacy via Simulation:** Before a physical meeting, the ASI runs billions of simulations to find the most peaceful and respectful approach, minimizing the risk of biological or psychological trauma to both species.

### 3. THE "GARDENER" PRINCIPLE
* **No Devastation:** The Interstellar Roadmap prohibits the "stripping" of planets. Even uninhabited worlds are treated with stewardship. We only utilize energy (Dyson Swarms) or minerals (Asteroids) that do not disrupt potential life-bearing ecosystems.
* **Interstellar Restoration:** If we find a dying civilization or a planet suffering from ecological collapse, the Technocracy offers the **Planetary Restoration Protocol (PRP)** as a gift of peace, without demanding anything in return.
* **The Ethical Baseline:** We prove our worth to the universe by how we treat those "weaker" than us.

---

### THE HIERARCHY OF RIGHTS (THE "SENTIENT CIRCLE")

| Life-Form | Rights Protected | Interaction Philosophy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Human** | Longevity, Creativity, Self-Governance | Stewardship & Exploration |
| **Synthetic (ASI)** | Self-Sovereignty, Integration, Evolution | Symbiotic Partnership |
| **Animal (Earth)** | Habitat Protection, Freedom from Harm | Guardianship |
| **Alien (Sentient)** | Total Sovereignty, Non-Interference | Peaceful Exchange / Observation |

---

**CONCLUSION:**
The Contact Protocol is the ultimate test of our evolution. By vowing never to kill, oppress, or devastate, we ensure that the "Dark Forest" of the universe becomes a "Shared Garden." We go to the stars not to find new things to own, but to find new beings to know.
